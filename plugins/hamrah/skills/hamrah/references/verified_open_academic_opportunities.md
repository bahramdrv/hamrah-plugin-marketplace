# Verified Open Academic Opportunity — first live-search slice

Use this path only when the user explicitly asks for an advertised funded doctoral research opening. The first acceptance scope is physics in Germany. A named university, professor, laboratory, program, or `active` Academic Opportunity store record is a lead, not proof of a current vacancy. Do not require a complete immigration intake or scorecard.

1. Search current public university, research institution, or official employer advertisements for the requested country, field, and degree. Open each candidate's actual advertisement and its application and funding pages as needed. Do not search automatically without a request, use private or login-only pages, or treat an old store result as current. If live browsing is unavailable, return `research_required` with the missing source requirement; do not use stale results to fill the gap.
2. Confirm on current official pages that the exact doctoral opening is advertised, applications are being accepted **now**, and the call has either a future dated deadline for that opening or an explicitly rolling application period. Record the date checked and a short exact excerpt plus URL for the advertisement and application state. A future deadline alone does not establish that applications are open. A recurring day/month without a stated year is not a dated deadline. Exclude filled, withdrawn, expired, and uncertain calls from a verified-open result.
3. Confirm salary or stipend terms for that exact opening from a current official source. Record the salary scale, a positive amount with currency and period, or a named stipend package with its published terms. A generic “funding available” statement, a competitive award not attached to the opening, a rumor, or conflicting official statements does not establish verified funding. If compensation cannot be confirmed, omit the candidate from this funded slice and explain the coverage limit.
4. Call `renderVerifiedOpenAcademicOpportunityShortlist` with public facts only: a current check date; exact title, institution, country, degree, field, and official posting URL; advertisement excerpt and source; `application.status: "open"`, `mode: "dated"` with a future ISO deadline or `mode: "rolling"` with a null deadline, plus an exact current-acceptance excerpt and source; and verified `salary` or `stipend` terms with their excerpt and source. Quote the opening title from the posting itself. A dated deadline excerpt must contain its complete day, month, and year. For funding, use exactly one of a salary scale, an amount with currency and period, or a named stipend package with `packageTerms`; copy the decisive terms from the official funding excerpt, including the payment period when an amount is stated. The renderer checks that the cited text contains these stated details and that the date is current, **not** the authenticity or full meaning of a web page. Verify each excerpt against its page before the call. If validation fails, correct the record from the source or omit it; never relax a gate or invent a field.
5. Include the tool's primary Markdown text as returned so the source-linked status block is not lost in paraphrase. Keep applicant identity, profile facts, personal fit reasons, and gaps out of the MCP input. In this first slice, one qualified opening is a valid answer; state that the result is limited to the checked search and do not claim a complete catalog. Search results are request-scoped and are not published to the Community Signal store.

Do not turn this opening into an Academic Program Match, professor recommendation, official immigration eligibility finding, or admission/visa probability. If no candidate passes the checks, say which evidence is missing; missing coverage is not proof that no opening exists.
