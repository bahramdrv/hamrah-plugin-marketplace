# Matching contract

Each result identifies the exact institution, degree/program title, degree level, requested research field, country, official program URL, and check date. A program page establishes a program, not a funded opening or supervisor availability. Verify separate official pages when program, admissions, tuition, scholarships, and deadlines are not co-located. Return 3–5 programs by default, or fewer with a coverage explanation when evidence is thin.

Match explicit profile facts and record prerequisites and unknowns as gaps. A minimal search can use country, degree, and field while identifying missing language, intake, and funding facts that change fit. Search external sources with only the terms needed for discovery; keep personal profile comparison in Hamrah. Normalize the requested field label in `search_scope` so each shortlisted program's `field` matches it; this field is a search label, not a claim that the institution uses that exact field name. Show the institution's published research areas and the strength of their overlap in source-linked `match_reasons`; mark indirect overlap as a gap. A different requested field requires a separate user request. Keep these layers separate:

- `admission_fit`: academic background, GPA scale, prerequisite courses, research alignment, portfolio, and language.
- `affordability`: tuition currency/period, living-cost evidence, deposits, and applicant budget.
- `funding`: published terms and whether funding is guaranteed, competitive, unavailable, or unknown.
- `immigration_context`: connection to a previously assessed route; this is not a new eligibility decision.
- `preference_match`: stated preferences; this cannot change admission or immigration fit.
- `iranian_evidence`: a source's explicit statement about an Iranian applicant's nationality condition; absent a statement, use `unknown` rather than assuming permission or restriction.

Record `admissions.application` separately from its deadline. Use `open`, `not_yet_open`, or `closed` only when an official admissions page confirms that status for a named intake; otherwise use `unknown`. When both are known, the application status and deadline must refer to the same intake. A closed call may have an unknown next deadline. Deadline values require an intake/year and distinguish admission, scholarship, and supervisor-contact deadlines. In the exported program record, `admissions.deadline` is the admission deadline; scholarship and supervisor-contact dates belong in the relevant details and sources. A verified or expired admission deadline needs a date, intake, and official source URL. Compare its date with `checked_at`: use `expired` when the deadline is before the check date, and `verified` otherwise. A future deadline alone does not establish that applications are open. An unknown deadline remains `null`. For research degrees, record whether a supervisor is required and link to the department/faculty source; a faculty profile is not proof of an open position.

Use `program_matches_schema.json` version 3.0 as the export contract. `match_status` and its source-linked `match_reasons` describe academic admission fit only; affordability, funding, immigration context, and preferences stay separate. Link every recorded admissions requirement to its official source. `affordability.tuition` records the published amount, currency, period, and source separately from funding; `none` uses amount zero with an official source and may omit currency and period, while `unknown` has a null amount. `funding.status` is `verified` for published noncompetitive terms, `competitive` for a published competition, `none` only when an official source explicitly says none, and `unknown` otherwise. `iranian_evidence.status` is `explicit_permission`, `explicit_restriction`, or `unknown`; the two explicit values require a linked official source and explanation. These statuses describe the program's stated conditions, not admission or visa probability. Each claim source URL also appears in `sources`. Keep unknown values as `null` and explain material unknowns in `gaps` or `warnings`. Export only on request and validate the file before delivery.
