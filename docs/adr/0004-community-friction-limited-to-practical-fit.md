# Limit community friction to Practical Fit

The merged scorecard flow lets validated community signals, including Private Community Evidence from de-identified exports, lower a route's Practical Fit by at most 20 points. The spec says private evidence cannot support a public route ranking, and `CONTEXT.md` defines Practical Fit as the applicant's route fit after applicable community friction. We keep the downside-only adjustment on Practical Fit, because an applicant's own scorecard is context and warning rather than a public ranking. The adjustment must never enter the Iranian Route Viability Index, a Route Evidence Threshold, Rankable Route status, or viable route discovery ordering, and a scorecard must show it as a separate, labelled component. If Practical Fit is ever used to compare routes across applicants or published as a ranking, this decision must be revisited.
