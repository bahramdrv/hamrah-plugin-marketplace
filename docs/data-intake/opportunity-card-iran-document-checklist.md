# Opportunity Card document checklist for applicants in Iran — 26 September 2026

## Recheck — 27 September 2026

The [Tehran embassy document-list page](https://teheran.diplo.de/ir-de/02-service/2426568-2426568) still lists no Opportunity Card-specific Merkblatt. The [national-visa page](https://teheran.diplo.de/ir-de/02-service/2403868-2403868) names the Opportunity Card in the "NEW National visa Other" waitlist but gives no route-specific document list. The [federal Opportunity Card FAQ](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card/questions-answers) still defers the complete document list to the competent mission. This recheck does not resolve Q1 or Q2 below. The next evidence source is a written reply from the Tehran visa section or a published Tehran Opportunity Card checklist. See [the follow-up ticket](../../.scratch/opportunity-card-iran-documents/issues/01-obtain-tehran-document-requirements.md) for a precise enquiry and publication criteria.

This pass looked for a current, Opportunity Card-specific (Chancenkarte, § 20a AufenthG) document checklist for applicants who normally live in Iran. It aimed to settle the two questions left unresolved in `germany-answer-review.md`: transcripts (qst_17b48540d670fd552ec96ef472539596) and which education levels need legalisation (qst_cad2045f8511716b781f8a9d4d0eb0d1). All sources were checked on 26 September 2026. The German Embassy Tehran does not publish an Opportunity Card Merkblatt in German or Persian. Its document page lists Merkblätter for other purposes only. The embassy does place the Opportunity Card on the TLScontact "National visa Other" waitlist. The Federal Foreign Office Consular Services Portal lists Iran, but says online application is not available for the Tehran mission and sends applicants to apply on site. The federal Opportunity Card FAQ lists generic document types and leaves the full list to the competent embassy. No official source found here says whether an Opportunity Card applicant from Iran must translate and legalise transcripts, or legalise every education level. Both questions stay unresolved. Checklists for other visa categories at the same embassy, and the Opportunity Card checklist of another mission, are recorded separately below. They do not establish the Tehran Opportunity Card answer.

## Other findings

- **How to apply from Iran.** The Consular Services Portal lists Iran but shows Tehran as "Online application currently not available". Its instruction for that case is to apply on site, and its FAQ names a paper application as the alternative. The embassy routes the Opportunity Card through the TLScontact waitlist "NEW National visa Other (e.g. Chancenkarte; …)". Applicants register there and apply in person at the appointment. The page says the Federal Office for Foreign Affairs (BfAA) processes study and skilled-immigration files. It does not say explicitly whether the Opportunity Card falls in that group. The restricted-operations notice says registration on the new waitlists has been possible since 15 July 2026. It also says the timing of appointments for these waitlists is not yet foreseeable. So, as of 26 September 2026, there is no official online route for the Opportunity Card from Iran and no announced appointment date for in-person filing.
- **Legalisation timing.** Documents that need legalisation must be legalised before the visa appointment. Legalisation at the visa section on the day is no longer offered. The same notice lets family members of fast-track (§ 81a) applicants hand in pre-certified documents for legalisation together with the visa file. That concession is not extended to the Opportunity Card waitlist.
- **Translations.** Across purposes, the embassy's general rule is translation into German. The legalisation procedure requires a translator on the Iranian foreign ministry's list, then pre-certification by the Iranian justice and foreign ministries. The skilled-worker and Blue Card checklists (analogous only) state German translations pre-certified by the Iranian foreign ministry. The federal FAQ says to check whether a German translation of the degree is needed. For experience evidence, it accepts German or English originals or translations. No Tehran Opportunity Card source says whether English translations are acceptable.
- **Anabin/ZAB and legalisation.** The official sources treat Anabin and the ZAB statement as proof that the degree is recognised or comparable. None says either one replaces legalising or authenticating Iranian originals for the visa. ZAB says the competent embassy decides whether Anabin information is enough. ZAB's own "simple scan" rule applies only to its own procedure. Treat them as separate requirements unless the embassy says otherwise.
