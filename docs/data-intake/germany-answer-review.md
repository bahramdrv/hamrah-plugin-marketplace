## Question dispositions

| Question | Result | Basis and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work or insurance history for skilled worker route | Partially answered | The embassy checklist asks for professional-history evidence and names salary and social-insurance records as examples. It does not establish a universal minimum number of years or that every applicant must have an insurance record. Do not substitute the distinct professionally experienced worker visa. |
| Language evidence for skilled worker route | Official answer | The embassy accepts an employer confirmation of adequate German or English, may ask for certificates, and permits an employer explanation of how work can begin without foreign-language skills. No one certificate applies to every applicant; profession-specific rules still matter. |
| Translate and legalize transcripts for Opportunity Card | Unresolved | The [embassy legalization page](https://teheran.diplo.de/ir-de/2402396-2402396) explains the procedure but does not establish which transcripts this visa application requires. |
| Apply for Opportunity Card from Iran | Official answer | The Tehran embassy explicitly lists an Opportunity Card national visa waitlist. The Consular Services Portal marks online application for the Tehran mission as unavailable and sends applicants to apply on site, and the embassy says appointment timing is not yet foreseeable (see the [Iran document checklist review](opportunity-card-iran-document-checklist.md)). Appointment availability remains a separate, changing question. |
| Anabin alone or ZAB/occupational recognition for skilled worker route | Official answer | The embassy checklist requires matching Anabin degree and institution records for non-regulated academic work; ZAB is needed when either is absent. Regulated or vocational work requires the competent recognition authority or professional licence. The applicant must identify their actual degree and occupation to select the branch. |
| Unrelated work experience for Opportunity Card | Partially answered | The federal guide awards experience points only for work related to the formal qualification after graduation. Other points and eligibility remain case-specific. |
| Bachelor or master for Opportunity Card | Partially answered | The federal FAQ includes both bachelor and master degrees as academic degrees and requires degree-specific recognition evidence. It gives no universal preference. Which degree is more useful for this applicant depends on the actual qualifications, Anabin/ZAB records, and points path. |
| Self-check score/email and next step | Official answer | The self-check is an eligibility aid, not a visa application. The federal guide points to applying afterward; the Tehran embassy lists the local national visa waitlist. |
| Degree/specialization absent from Anabin | Partially answered | The federal FAQ requires positive degree and institution ratings or a ZAB statement. It does not resolve whether the asker's listed specialization counts as the rated degree. |
| Legalize every education level or only chosen degree | Unresolved | The embassy's general legalization page does not settle the document set for this application. Check the current category-specific checklist or ask the mission. |

A follow-up pass on 26 September searched for a current, Opportunity Card-specific document checklist for applicants in Iran and found none; it is recorded in [opportunity-card-iran-document-checklist.md](opportunity-card-iran-document-checklist.md). The embassy's general [document page](https://teheran.diplo.de/ir-de/02-service/2426568-2426568) does not list an Opportunity Card checklist, so the two legalization and transcript questions remain unresolved rather than borrowing rules from its study-visa FAQ or other missions. Settling them needs a Tehran Opportunity Card checklist or a written answer from the embassy's visa section. Questions without direct, current support remain unresolved in the published dataset.
