# Hamrah Immigration Intelligence

Hamrah presents source-linked immigration evidence across destinations and routes. Its conclusions are limited by the evidence available for each result.

## Language

**Evidence Coverage**:
The extent to which a country and route have current, attributable evidence for a specific question. Missing coverage means the answer is unknown or requires research; it is not evidence that the route is unavailable.
_Avoid_: Route availability

**Route Fact Pack**:
A source-linked collection of route facts and coverage results assembled for one route at one retrieval point. It can be partial when a contributing source fails or times out.
_Avoid_: Complete route assessment

**Iranian Route Viability Index**:
An evidence-based assessment of a route's practical usability for an Iranian applicant profile, reported with its own evidence confidence. A numeric value can be shown with low confidence when recent verified Iranian examples are absent; it is not an approval probability.
_Avoid_: Visa chance, official success rate, applicant fit

**Official Approval Statistic**:
An authority's own published count of applications and approvals or refusals for a defined population and period, from which Hamrah reproduces a rate only when numerator and denominator match. Community samples and Lived Experience counts can never supply one.
_Avoid_: Community success rate, visa chance

**Practical Fit**:
The existing scorecard measure of an applicant's overall route fit after applicable community friction. It remains separate from the Iranian Route Viability Index.
_Avoid_: Iranian Route Viability Index

**Rankable Route**:
A route with enough current official and Iran-specific evidence to compare against other routes. A provisional viability number alone does not make a route rankable.
_Avoid_: Route with a numeric score

**Route Evidence Threshold**:
A versioned, route-specific minimum for current, independent evidence needed before a route becomes rankable. A route without a defined threshold remains unranked.
_Avoid_: Universal success-count rule

**Source Authority**:
The degree to which a source can establish a particular kind of claim. Classification is governed by changeable, versioned rules, and an unrecognized source has unknown authority.
_Avoid_: Domain appearance, source title

**Provisional Assessment**:
A low-confidence interpretation that can guide further research without establishing official eligibility. It cannot turn an unverified requirement into an official pass or fail.
_Avoid_: Official eligibility result

**Evidence Candidate**:
A proposed question, claim, opportunity, experience, or source interpretation awaiting deterministic validation before publication.
_Avoid_: Published evidence

**Academic Opportunity**:
A source-linked record of a specific academic opening with its own evidence, verification state, and freshness. A mention of a university or professor alone does not establish an opening.
_Avoid_: University listing, professor profile

**Verified Open Academic Opportunity**:
An advertised Academic Opportunity whose current official source explicitly confirms that applications are being accepted at the check date, through a future deadline or a rolling call. A record marked active without that confirmation, or with unknown or expired application status, is not a verified open opportunity.
_Avoid_: Active dataset record, available program

**Academic Program Match**:
A request-scoped comparison of a named admissions program with an applicant's stated academic goals and relevant profile facts, backed by a current official program page. It identifies fit and gaps without implying admission or an open funded position.
_Avoid_: Admission chance, professor match, open position

**Academic Admission Call**:
A named admissions intake with its own application window and official conditions. A program listing alone does not establish that its current intake is open.
_Avoid_: Academic Program Match, university listing

**Academic Funding Call**:
A scholarship or stipend application with its own eligibility, terms, and application window. Being eligible to apply does not establish that an applicant has been awarded funding.
_Avoid_: Funded admission, guaranteed scholarship

**Academic Supervisor Lead**:
A request-scoped, source-linked professor or research-group contact prospect whose official page documents relevant research. Student recruitment and a former Iranian-student connection have separate evidence states; neither follows from research relevance alone.
_Avoid_: Academic Program Match, Verified Open Academic Opportunity, available supervisor

**Public Person Evidence**:
A public source that explicitly documents a person's connection to Iran and a relevant immigration or academic milestone. A reviewed source URL may be retained while the person's name is omitted from ordinary results; names, appearance, and language alone do not establish an Iranian connection.
_Avoid_: Inferred nationality, success anecdote

**Private Community Evidence**:
A de-identified observation whose original source cannot be publicly inspected. It may supply context or a warning, but it does not support a public route ranking.
_Avoid_: Public Person Evidence, independently verifiable evidence
