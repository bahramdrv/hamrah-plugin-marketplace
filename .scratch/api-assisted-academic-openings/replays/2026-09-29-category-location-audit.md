# Category and primary-location audit — 2026-09-29

- Ai2's anonymous Greenhouse board returned 19 jobs. The research and Young Investigator records inspected used primary `location.name: "Seattle, WA"` and an office location of `Seattle, WA, United States`. The mapper now requires these to agree before reporting US; an office without a matching primary location is excluded.
- A public TRI Lever replay for `robotics` returned “Postdoctoral Researcher, Human Aware Interaction Learning” in both `postdoc` and `research_job`. This was a category error. The general research-job title filter now excludes explicit postdoctoral, predoctoral, internship, and Young Investigator titles. The UK Faculty Ashby filter applies the same exclusion.
- After the fix, a live US `robotics` research-job replay returned Ai2 “Research Scientist, Robotics” and TRI general research roles, with no explicit postdoctoral title. Source requests succeeded. All discovery candidates remain `unverified` pending exact official posting and application checks.
- EMBL's public [job snippet API](https://www.embl.org/api/v1/pattern.html?filter-content-type=jobs&filter-field-date-after%5Bfield_jobs_expiration%5D=today&limit=10&pattern=vf-jobs-snippet&sort-field-value%5Bcreated%5D=DESC&source=contenthub) was assessed as another possible publisher source. Its current response is HTML and showed no specifically titled postdoctoral vacancy in fifteen records; it was not connected in this slice.
