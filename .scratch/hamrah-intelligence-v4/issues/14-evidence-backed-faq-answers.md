# 14 — Evidence-backed FAQ answers

**What to build:** An applicant can ask a frequent community question and receive a cited answer that separates official rules from community observations, or an explicit unresolved result.

Blocked by: 13 — Question extraction and retrieval

Status: done

**Phase:** 3

- [ ] answerCommunityQuestion gathers relevant official and community evidence, checks scope, contradictions, freshness, and independence, and returns answer type, confidence, citations, and last verification time.
- [ ] An official rule is labeled official only when its source authority and freshness pass; copied community reports do not become multiple sources.
- [ ] Missing, stale, or conflicting support yields partially answered, outdated, or unresolved without an invented conclusion.
- [ ] MCP tests cover official, community-only, contradictory, and research-required answers.
