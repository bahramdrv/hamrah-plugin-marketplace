# 15 — Route Claim retrieval

**What to build:** A researcher can publish a Route Claim separately from a Signal, and a facilitator can find it with its applicant scope and supporting and opposing evidence.

Blocked by: 14 — Evidence-backed FAQ answers

Status: done

**Phase:** 4

- [ ] Route Claims represent official rules, operational or anecdotal patterns, opportunities, risks, and workarounds with country, route, stage, and applicant scope.
- [ ] searchRouteClaims returns relevant claims with stable IDs and enough evidence detail for inspection, without treating a claim as a fact.
- [ ] Responses preserve supporting, opposing, public and private evidence classes, verification status, and dates.
- [ ] Dataset-to-MCP tests show scope filtering and empty coverage without fabricated claims.
