# 11 — Cross-dataset aggregation

**What to build:** A facilitator can search one route across multiple validated datasets and see clustered, non-duplicated evidence with honest coverage and contradiction information.

Blocked by: 09 — Version 2 and 3 migration; 10 — Validated publication and stable IDs

Status: done

**Phase:** 2

- [ ] Cross-posted or copied records share an evidence cluster and independence group rather than counting as separate reports.
- [ ] The response preserves source family, copy risk, time window, supporting and opposing evidence, and dataset coverage.
- [ ] Historical, stale, and superseded records remain auditable but cannot become current support by default.
- [ ] A multi-dataset search test demonstrates one independent count for copies and retention of contradictory records.
