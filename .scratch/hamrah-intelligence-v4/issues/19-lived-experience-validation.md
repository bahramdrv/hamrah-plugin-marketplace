# 19 — Lived Experience validation

**What to build:** A researcher can publish a minimal Iranian Lived Experience only when public evidence explicitly supports the Iran connection and a route milestone or failure.

Blocked by: 18 — Opportunity verification

Status: done

**Phase:** 6

- [ ] Self-declared or explicitly documented Iran context is accepted; names, appearance, language, and ambiguous education history alone leave Iranian status unknown.
- [ ] Study, work, and settlement successes require their defined attained milestones; merely applying is not counted as success.
- [ ] Negative outcomes and delays retain country, route, entity, applicant scope, event date, and source links.
- [ ] Privacy validation permits a necessary public profile URL only under a narrow provenance exception and removes unnecessary person details.
- [ ] Validation tests cover positive, ambiguous, negative, and privacy-unsafe candidates.
