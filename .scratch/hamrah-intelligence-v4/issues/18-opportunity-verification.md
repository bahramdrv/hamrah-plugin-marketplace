# 18 — Opportunity verification

**What to build:** An applicant can distinguish a current, source-backed academic opening from an expired deadline or a funding rumor.

Blocked by: 17 — Academic Opportunity retrieval

Status: done

**Phase:** 5

- [ ] Admission deadlines and funding facts are verified against the appropriate official institution source and fact-type freshness policy.
- [ ] Expired deadlines are stale or historical, while unverified stipend, waiver, assistantship, or nationality restrictions remain unknown.
- [ ] Conflicting official and community information is preserved with confidence and cannot make unsupported funding definite.
- [ ] Publication-to-MCP tests cover current, expired, unverified, and contradictory opportunities.
