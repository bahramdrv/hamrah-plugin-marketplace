# 17 — Academic Opportunity retrieval

**What to build:** An applicant can search and retrieve Academic Opportunities as separate artifacts by institution, program, degree, field, funding, or deadline.

Blocked by: 16 — Claim validation and Evidence Confidence

Status: done

**Phase:** 5

- [ ] Opportunity records expose institution, department or program, degree and field, research area or supervisor when relevant, funding components, admission conditions, Iranian evidence status, provenance, and freshness.
- [ ] searchAcademicOpportunities and getAcademicOpportunity return only validated records with stable IDs and explicit coverage.
- [ ] Filters preserve route and country scope; missing fields remain unknown rather than inferred from community discussion.
- [ ] MCP tests demonstrate search, detail retrieval, and no-data behavior.
