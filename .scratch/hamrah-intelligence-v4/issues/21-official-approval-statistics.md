# 21 — Separate official approval statistics

**What to build:** An applicant can see official approval statistics separately from community observations when an authority publishes a matching population, period, numerator, and denominator.

Blocked by: 20 — Experience search and observed cases

Status: done

**Phase:** 7

- [ ] A statistic requires an authoritative URL, defined population and period, applications, approvals or refusals, and internally consistent arithmetic.
- [ ] Missing denominator, mismatched population, and community sample ratios cannot populate official_success_rate.
- [ ] A route assessment or statistics retrieval presents the statistic with its source and period separately from IRVI, applicant fit, and Community Confidence.
- [ ] Tests cover valid official data and rejected or unresolved incomplete statistics.
